# NU Launcher by AQ

A minimalist, distraction-free Android launcher built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, featuring an edge-to-edge liquid glass aesthetic.

NU Launcher is designed to eliminate digital clutter and reduce mindless phone pickups. By surfacing only essential communications—**Phone**, **Messaging**, and **WhatsApp**—alongside a clean digital clock and mindful focus reminders, NU Launcher keeps your device intentional and calm.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Essential-First Layout**: Direct access to core communications (Phone, Messaging, WhatsApp) without distraction or clutter.
- **Liquid Glassmorphic UI**: Translucent frosted surfaces with specular lighting accents and smooth tactile haptics.
- **Low-Light & OLED Optimization**:
  - **Pitch Black OLED** mode (`#000000`) for maximum night readability and battery preservation.
  - **Liquid Dark**, **Liquid Dusk**, and **Frosted Light** themes.
  - **High-Contrast Low-Light Mode** to enhance text legibility in dark environments.
- **Minimalist Clock & Date**: Clean typography supporting both 12-hour and 24-hour formats.
- **Mindful Focus Prompts**: Rotating digital intention cues to encourage intentional usage.
- **Quick Controls**: Access flashlight, sound profile, and quick settings with a swipe or tap.
- **100% Offline & Private**: Zero internet permissions, zero analytics, zero background tracking.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Language**: Kotlin
- **UI Toolkit**: Jetpack Compose (Material Design 3)
- **Architecture**: MVVM with Kotlin Coroutines & StateFlow
- **Minimum SDK**: Android 8.0 (API 26)
- **Target SDK**: Android 14 / 15 (API 34 / 35)

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- [Android Studio](https://developer.android.com/studio) (Ladybug 2024.2+ recommended)
- JDK 17 or JDK 21

### Opening the Project
1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open Android Studio and select **Open**.
3. Navigate to the downloaded project folder and click **OK**.
4. Allow Gradle to download dependencies and sync the project.
5. Click **Run** (`Shift + F10`) to deploy to your device or emulator.
