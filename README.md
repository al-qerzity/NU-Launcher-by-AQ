# NU Launcher by AQ

> A minimalist, distraction-free Android launcher built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, featuring an edge-to-edge liquid glass aesthetic.

NU Launcher is designed to eliminate digital clutter and reduce mindless phone screen time. By surfacing only your core essentials—Phone, Messaging, and WhatsApp—alongside a clean digital clock and mindful focus reminders, NU Launcher keeps your home screen elegant, quiet, and intentional.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Essential-First Layout**: Direct access to core communications (Phone, Messages, WhatsApp) without the distraction of an overwhelming app grid.
- **Liquid Glassmorphic UI**: Translucent frosted surfaces with specular lighting accents and smooth tactile haptics.
- **Low-Light & OLED Optimization**:
  - **Pitch Black OLED** mode (`#000000`) for maximum night readability and battery preservation.
  - **Liquid Dark**, **Liquid Dusk**, and **Frosted Light** glass themes.
  - **High-Contrast Low-Light Mode** to enhance text legibility in dark environments.
- **Minimalist Clock & Date**: Clean typography supporting both 12-hour and 24-hour formats.
- **Mindful Focus Prompts**: Rotating digital intention cues to encourage intentional usage.
- **100% Offline & Private**: Zero internet permissions, zero analytics, and zero tracking.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

- **Language**: [Kotlin](https://kotlinlang.org/)
- **UI Framework**: [Jetpack Compose](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/compose) (Material 3)
- **State & Architecture**: MVVM, Kotlin Coroutines, StateFlow
- **Storage**: Encrypted / Key-Value SharedPreferences
- **Target SDK**: Android 14+ (API 34/35)
